Where automation can help

What could your team automate?

Each scenario is a service scope to assess during discovery. Data quality, access and acceptance checks determine what can be delivered. The case pages show which parts are demonstrated and which remain proposed.

SOP & technical knowledge assistance

Staff search folders and ask colleagues for the right procedure.

Inputs: approved SOPs, manuals and inspection specifications.

Pilot deliverable: a searchable collection with source-linked results, document versions and clear missing-evidence responses.

Document & table processing

Teams retype fields from PDFs, scans and spreadsheets.

Inputs: one document family, sample files and an agreed field list.

Pilot deliverable: structured records, source locations and a review queue for uncertain or missing fields.

Operational reporting & analysis

Recurring reports require manual reconciliation across spreadsheets.

Inputs: approved order, delivery or inspection records and agreed metric definitions.

Pilot deliverable: repeatable calculations, exception summaries and supporting records that a reviewer can check.

Supplier follow-up & exception workflows

Late deliveries and quality issues get lost across email and spreadsheets.

Inputs: supplier updates, order references, exception rules and responsible owners.

Pilot deliverable: an exception list, assigned actions and notification or work-order drafts. Connected writes require explicit authorization and review.

Part & batch traceability

Teams need to identify which records are connected to an affected part or batch.

Inputs: supplier, part, batch and product relationships with reliable source identifiers.

Pilot deliverable: a bounded traceability query with inspectable source records and a clear statement of missing relationships.

AI workflow quality improvement

An existing assistant returns weak evidence or fails during tool calls.

Inputs: a reproducible system version, representative questions and approved test access.

Review deliverable: reproducible failures, citation and tool checks, prioritized fixes and a retest plan.

Private knowledge & system integration

A useful AI workflow still needs to fit company identity, data and infrastructure rules.

Inputs: an approved environment, identity source and one system interface.

PoC deliverable: a bounded connection and deployment plan with permission, recovery and handoff checks.

Five bounded starting points

Start with a defined pilot.

Knowledge retrieval and document processing are natural starting points. Graph retrieval and greater autonomy are optional extensions after simpler approaches are evaluated.
